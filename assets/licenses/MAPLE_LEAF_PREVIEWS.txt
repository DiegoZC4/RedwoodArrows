Source: https://www.mapleleafpress.com/
Full per-image URLs, dimensions, extraction provenance and SHA-256 hashes are
bundled in assets/target-previews/manifest.json. Retrieved October 8, 2026.

Purpose: offline identification in Diego's personal experimental app. These are
small catalog thumbnails, not print-ready target sheets or digitized scoring
templates. This project does not assert a general redistribution license for
the artwork. Obtain permission before a public/commercial release.

95 previews preserve the maker's original HTML JPEG bytes. Two complete embedded
illustrations were extracted from the maker's PDF flyers with Poppler; the PDF
files themselves are not bundled. No artwork is used to set measurement scale.
