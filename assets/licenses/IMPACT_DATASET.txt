2a249f3d8a3c35badaa80548a17e3085ee53ae0c7e4167b9241ffdf567e81078

The experimental model is locally adapted using these point annotations,
private assistant-reviewed photographs, synthetic arrow masks, and a pretrained
torchvision MobileNetV3-Small backbone. Training adds a new entry-center decoder.
Dataset photographs and private annotations are NOT included in the APK.

This is a noncommercial experimental model release. The project-specific
entry-model adaptation is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, retaining the
dataset's attribution, noncommercial and share-alike terms:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
No endorsement by the dataset author is implied. Changes include the new
decoder, fine-tuning and a two-model ensemble; hashes and evaluation details
are in impact/model-card.json. No training photographs or annotations are
distributed. This notice does not grant rights beyond the upstream terms.

The backbone was adapted from torchvision's ImageNet-pretrained
MobileNetV3-Small. Its code notice is in TORCHVISION.txt. Pretrained weights
can have training-data terms separate from the code license; review these
before other uses, especially commercial redistribution:
https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models.html#general-information-on-pre-trained-weights

No claim of certified physical accuracy or complete detection of occluded
arrows is made. Multi-view agreement is not an independent accuracy guarantee.
